Notes
-----
All invariant calculations used backbone-rigid-invariant 1.2.2.2.

Experimental BRI and LAI comparisons use the prediction ensemble median,
with the 5th-95th percentile interval.

BTI experimental comparisons use signed circular angular differences so
that the -180/180 degree boundary is handled correctly.

Boundary or otherwise undefined invariant values may produce NaNs. These
are excluded from feature-level error metrics.
