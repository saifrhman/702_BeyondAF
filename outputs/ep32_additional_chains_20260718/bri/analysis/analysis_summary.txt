7DSQ-B
-------
Predictions analysed: 100 relaxed structures
Sequence-matched residues used: 464
Experimental reference: 7DSQ chain B

Important data audit finding:
The inherited FASTA header identifies the target as 7dsq_A, but the
464-residue sequence matches experimental 7DSQ chain B with 100% sequence
identity and 100% coverage. It does not match chain A. The historical
prediction filenames therefore retain the inherited 7dsq_A label, but
experimental comparisons must use chain B.

Outputs:
- BRI_comparison.png
- LAI_comparison.png
- BTI_comparison.png
- feature_error_summary.csv
- per_residue_invariant_summary.csv

Notes
-----
All invariant calculations used backbone-rigid-invariant 1.2.2.2.

Boundary or locally undefined invariant values produce NaNs for a small
number of residues. These were excluded from feature-level error metrics.

BTI torsion comparisons use signed circular angular differences to handle
the -180/180 degree wraparound correctly.
