7DSQ target-chain assignment

The inherited FASTA file is named 7dsq.fasta and has the header 7dsq_A.
However, its 464-residue sequence matches experimental PDB entry 7DSQ
chain B exactly and does not match chain A.

The OpenFold predictions and precomputed alignments stored under the
historical label 7dsq_A therefore correspond biologically to 7DSQ chain B.

For all experimental comparisons:
- use 7dsq_B_experimental.pdb
- use 7dsq_B_experimental.csv
- do not use 7dsq_A_experimental.csv

The predictions do not require rerunning because their sequence matches
chain B with 100% identity and coverage.
